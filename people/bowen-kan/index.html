About

Bowen Kan is a PhD student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. His work focuses on large-scale parallel quantum chemistry based on QiankunNet, bringing high-performance computing and neural-network quantum states together for electronic-structure calculations.
