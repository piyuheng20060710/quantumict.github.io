About

Muhammad Naeem Tahir develops accurate and efficient first-principles electronic-structure methods in FHI-aims. His research spans density functional theory, random-phase approximation, analytical gradients, and scalable algorithms for energies and forces in molecular and condensed-phase systems.

  • Random Phase Approximation
  • Analytical Gradients
