About

Hao Wu studies ab initio methods for strongly correlated electronic systems. His work combines neural-network quantum states with auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo, with particular interest in how many-body trial wave functions affect accuracy for transition-metal and f-electron systems.

Research Interests

  • Strongly Correlated Electrons
  • Auxiliary-Field Quantum Monte Carlo
