Quantum Physics · Chemistry · HPC

Accelerating scientific discovery through computation.

We develop scalable methods and intelligent tools for quantum chemistry, first-principles simulation, and high-performance scientific computing.

01

Research focus

From physical principles to extreme-scale computation.

01

Neural Quantum States

Transformer and tensor-network representations for molecular and solid-state electronic structure.

02

First-Principles Methods

Method development for response properties, dynamics, and electron–phonon interactions.

03

Scientific HPC

Scalable algorithms and software for quantum simulation, molecular dynamics, and kinetic Monte Carlo.

02

Flagship research program

QiankunNet

A family of Transformer-based neural-network quantum-state methods for solving molecular and materials problems—from wave functions to forces, embedding, potential-energy surfaces, and high-performance configuration interaction.

Original roadmap ↗
2023

Foundation

QiankunNet

A generative Transformer wave-function ansatz brings attention mechanisms and autoregressive sampling into quantum chemistry.
Nov 2023

Scale

QiankunNet–High Performance

Distributed sampling and parallel local-energy evaluation extend neural quantum states to large active spaces and HPC systems.View publication ↗
Jul 2024

Materials

QiankunNet–Solid

The Transformer NNQS framework is extended to periodic systems and electronic band structures of real solids.View publication ↗
Sep 2024

Embedding

QiankunNet–DMET

Neural quantum states and density matrix embedding theory are combined for strongly correlated materials.View publication ↗
Oct 2024

Properties

QiankunNet–Force

Accurate interatomic forces are evaluated with a generative Transformer architecture and variational quantum Monte Carlo.View publication ↗
Mar 2025

Tensor networks

QiankunNet–DMRG

Transformer neural networks and tensor-network states are bridged to improve accuracy and convergence in strongly correlated systems.View publication ↗
Jul 2025

Molecular dynamics

QiankunNet–PES

A fast, scalable NNQS workflow maps molecular potential-energy surfaces while retaining ab initio accuracy.View publication ↗
Sep 2025

Nature Communications

Core framework published

QiankunNet is formally presented as a general Transformer-based framework for the many-electron Schrödinger equation.View publication ↗
Nov 2025

NNQS–SCI

Adaptive neural quantum states tackle trillion-dimensional Hilbert spaces with scalable configuration selection.View publication ↗

QiankunNet–cuSCI
